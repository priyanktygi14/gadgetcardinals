# GadgetCartels.in — Content Direction

## Brand Voice

Voice:

- confident
- playful
- energetic
- direct
- youthful
- slightly rebellious
- premium without sounding corporate

Avoid overly formal ecommerce copy.

## Product Content

Do not invent:

- technical specifications
- warranty promises
- shipping promises
- payment claims
- customer numbers

Use placeholders or CMS/API data until verified.

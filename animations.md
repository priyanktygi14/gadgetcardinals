# GadgetCartels.in — Animation System

## Philosophy

Animation should make the site feel alive without weakening the bold editorial composition.

Keep most interactions between 150ms and 300ms.

## Header

- navigation hover: subtle underline/opacity
- icon hover: scale 1.03–1.06
- menu button: slight background/scale response

## Hero

Optional:

- product image enters with subtle translate + opacity
- neon background shape gently shifts
- decorative dots/lines fade in
- badge can have extremely subtle rotation

Avoid dramatic continuous hero animations.

## Category Cards

On hover:

- image scale: 1.03–1.05
- arrow moves slightly
- card accent becomes more visually prominent

## Deal Cards

On hover:

- card lifts slightly
- product image scales subtly
- cart button responds
- discount badge remains readable

## Buttons

Primary button:

- subtle translateY(-1px)
- arrow moves 3–5px

Secondary button:

- border/opacity transition

## Carousel

Use smooth horizontal movement.

Do not make autoplay mandatory.

## Scroll Reveal

Optional:

- opacity 0 → 1
- translateY 16–24px
- duration 400–600ms

Respect:

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`

## Mobile

Reduce or disable expensive animation.

Prioritize responsiveness and tap interaction.

# GadgetCartels.in — Page Architecture

## Page 1 — Home

Route:

`/`

Sections:

1. Header
2. Hero
3. Category selector
4. Trust strip
5. Crazy Deals
6. Why Choose GadgetCartels
7. Testimonial
8. Newsletter
9. Footer

The homepage must match the supplied reference most closely.

---

## Page 2 — Shop

Route:

`/shop`

Purpose:

Product discovery.

Layout:

- same header
- large editorial page title
- category filters
- product grid
- optional sorting
- product cards using the homepage visual language
- pagination/load more
- same footer

Do not make this look like a generic marketplace.

---

## Page 3 — Collections

Route:

`/collections`

Show:

- iPhone
- MacBook
- iPad
- AirPods
- Apple Watch
- Accessories

Use large editorial collection tiles inspired by the homepage category strip.

---

## Page 4 — Product Detail

Route:

`/product/[slug]`

Structure:

- product gallery
- product title
- price
- old price
- discount
- product highlights
- CTA
- delivery/return information
- related products

Keep the product page visually bold and dark.

---

## Page 5 — About Us

Route:

`/about`

Use:

- bold editorial hero
- brand story
- values
- statistics
- visual blocks
- trust information

Avoid corporate-looking layouts.

---

## Page 6 — Contact

Route:

`/contact`

Include:

- contact headline
- short copy
- contact details
- contact form
- FAQ/help links
- footer

Use the same neon editorial language.

---

## Page 7 — Cart

Route:

`/cart`

Include:

- cart items
- quantities
- prices
- subtotal
- delivery information
- checkout CTA

Keep the page visually consistent with the dark/neon system.

---

## Page 8 — Search

Route:

`/search`

Include:

- search input
- result count
- product results
- empty state

Use the same visual language.

---
